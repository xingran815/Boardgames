# Spirits of the Wild — Quick Reference

**Goal:** collect stones with action cards and place them on your 5 animals. Most points wins.

**Setup:** 4 non-Spirit stones in the bowl (redraw clear ones) · 6 action cards face-up each · Spirit Power cards in 2 face-up piles of 3 · start player flips *Take Two Stones* face-down.

## Your turn

1. Pick **one face-up** action card (any stones it takes must be in the bowl).
2. Do it. Place taken stones on your board; unplaceable stones go back to the bowl.
3. Flip the card **face-down**.

## Action cards (6 per player)

| Card | Effect |
|---|---|
| Take One Stone (×2) | Take 1 from bowl. *Backs: half a Coyote each — both face-down = move Coyote to opponent.* |
| Take Two Stones | Take 2 from bowl (needs ≥ 2). |
| Add Two & Take One | Bag → bowl 2, then take 1. |
| Add Three & Move Coyote | Bag → bowl 3, then **may** put Coyote on an opponent's animal. |
| Spirit Power & Recover | Needs ≥ 3 other cards face-down. Use a top Spirit card, put it under its pile, flip **all 6** cards face-up. |

## Spirit Power cards

| Spirit | Effect |
|---|---|
| Bear | Bowl stones → bag, redraw same number into bowl, take 1. |
| Wolf | Bag → bowl 4, take 1. |
| Ram | Draw 2 from bag straight to your board (must place if possible; rest → bowl). |
| Eagle | Take 1; after recovering cards, **take another turn**. |
| Deer | Your board → bowl 1 stone, then take 2. |
| Bison | Bag → bowl 3, take 1, then return 1 bowl stone to the bag. |

## Animal scoring

| Animal | Requirement | Points |
|---|---|---|
| Rabbit | 3 different colours | 6 (complete only) |
| Turtle | all one colour | 1 / 3 / 6 / 9 / 14 for 1–5 stones |
| Salmon | all different colours | 1 / 2 / 4 / 6 / 9 / 13 for 1–6 stones |
| Owl | same-colour pairs | 4 per complete pair (3 in English 2017 ed.) |
| Beaver | 3-of-a-kind + a pair | 5 + 2, +7 bonus if both = 14 |

**Spirit Stone (clear):** only in an animal's bonus space · **doubles** that animal · **closes** the section · not a colour.

**Coyote:** you can't add any stones (incl. Spirit Stones) to the animal it sits on.

## End & ties

- End of any turn with **5 Spirit Stones out of the bag** (bowl + boards) → game ends at once. (Bison returning one in time keeps play going.)
- Highest total wins. **Tie:** player with the **Coyote on their board** wins.
