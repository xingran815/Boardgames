# Azul — Quick Reference

**Goal:** collect tiles, complete pattern lines, move tiles to your wall for points. The game ends after the round in which someone completes a horizontal wall row. Most points wins.

**Setup:** all players use the **same board side** (coloured; grey = variant) · score markers on 0 · factory displays: **5 / 7 / 9** for **2 / 3 / 4** players · 100 tiles (20 × B blue, Y yellow, R red, K black, W white) in the bag · starting player takes the marker and puts **4 tiles** on each factory display.

## Each round

**A. Factory offer** (starting player puts the marker in the centre and goes first; then clockwise)

1. Take **all tiles of one colour** from **one factory display** (push the rest into the centre) **or** from **the centre** (first to take from the centre also takes the starting player marker → leftmost floor space).
2. Put them all in **one pattern line**, right to left. One colour per line; not a colour already in that wall row. Extras → floor line. You may put them all on the floor line instead.
3. Phase ends when all factory displays **and** the centre are empty.

**B. Wall-tiling** (everyone at once, lines top to bottom)

- **Complete** line → rightmost tile to the same-colour space in that wall row, score it now; rest of the line → box lid.
- Incomplete lines stay as they are.

**C. Prepare** (if nobody has a full row): marker holder puts 4 tiles on each factory display. Bag empty → pour in the box lid and continue. Bag and lid both empty → start anyway.

## Scoring a new wall tile

- No neighbour across or down → **1**.
- Otherwise: tiles in its unbroken line **across** (if ≥ 2) **+** tiles in its unbroken line **down** (if ≥ 2).
- Examples: lone tile 1 · line of 3 across = 3 · across 4 and down 3 = **7**.

## Floor line (end of phase B)

| Items | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Spaces | −1 | −1 | −2 | −2 | −2 | −3 | −3 |
| Total lost | 1 | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 11 | 14 |

Starting player marker counts as an item (then keep it: you start next round) · tiles past space 7 → box lid, no extra cost · score never below 0.

## End of game

After phase B of the round in which **any player completes a horizontal row** (a full column doesn't end it). Bonuses:

| Each | Bonus |
|---|---|
| complete row (across) | **+2** |
| complete column (down) | **+7** |
| colour with all 5 tiles on the wall | **+10** |

**Tie:** more complete rows wins; still tied → shared victory.

## Grey-wall variant

Place each tile on **any empty space** of its row · each colour **once per column** (and once per row) · no allowed space → **all** tiles of that line go to the floor line.
